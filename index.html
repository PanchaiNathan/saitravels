Privacy Policy

At Sai Travel, accessible from our online booking portal, the privacy of our visitors and passengers is of extreme importance to us. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Sai Travel and how we use it.

Information We Collect

When you place a booking enquiry through our website or WhatsApp, we ask for contact information including name, telephone number, pickup and drop points, and date of travel. This information is utilized solely to provide transportation and dispatch vehicle details.

Data Security & Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to any third parties. Your details are strictly shared with your designated chauffeur to coordinate your ride on the date of travel.

Terms & Conditions
Booking Confirmation & Tariffs

All taxi reservations are confirmed based on vehicle availability. Kilometer reading commences and terminates in accordance with standard city garage/pickup regulations unless a fixed-rate one-way drop tariff is mutually agreed upon in writing.

Toll & Parking Charges

Highway toll taxes, state permits (for interstate journeys), and airport/railway parking fees are payable at actuals as incurred during your trip.

Passenger Conduct & Safety

Passengers are requested to adhere to local transport guidelines, wear seat belts, and refrain from hazardous activities during vehicle motion.

Cancellation & Refund Policy
Hassle-Free Cancellation

We understand that travel plans can change unexpectedly. You may cancel or reschedule your ride with zero penalty by notifying our dispatch team at least 2 hours prior to the scheduled pickup time for local trips, and 4 hours prior for outstation journeys.

Advance Deposits

For bookings where an advance deposit was paid for special holiday packages or multi-day peak rentals, refunds are processed promptly within 24 to 48 hours.
